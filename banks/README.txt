Bank logos (optional)
=====================
Put the official bank logos here as PNG files with a transparent background:

  ing.png          ING Bank
  bt.png           Banca Transilvania
  brd.png          BRD - Groupe Societe Generale
  cec.png          CEC Bank
  raiffeisen.png   Raiffeisen Bank
  revolut.png      Revolut

The app shows them on the cards automatically the next time it starts.
Without a file, the bank name is shown on the card instead.
In the Microsoft Store version the app folder is read-only; use
%USERPROFILE%\CardVault\banks instead.
Logos are registered trademarks of the banks; use them according to each
bank's brand guidelines.
